# Third-party licences

The project's own code is under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). The dashboard in `ui/` is
built into the desktop app and the Docker image, and ships the font and the icons below.

## Inter (font)

- Inter, variable font (weight axis, normal style), from the npm package
  `@fontsource-variable/inter`: WOFF2 files for the Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic,
  Cyrillic Extended, Greek, Greek Extended and Vietnamese subsets.
- Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter).
- Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, [text below](#sil-open-font-license-11).

## Lucide (icons)

  `ui/src/components/ui/Icon.svelte`.
- Copyright (c) 2026 Lucide Icons and Contributors. Licence: ISC,
  [text below](#isc-license-lucide).
- The Lucide icons derived from Feather are also Copyright (c) 2013-present Cole Bemis,
  under the MIT licence, [text below](#mit-license-feather).

## The author's artwork

The app icon (`ui/public/app-icon.svg`, the PNG icons and favicons in `ui/public/`,
`src-tauri/icons/`, `miner-core/assets/notify-test/logo.png`) and the logos of the

## Libraries

The Rust crates and npm packages compiled into the app are under permissive licences
(mostly MIT and Apache-2.0, also ISC, BSD, Zlib, Unicode-3.0 and similar), plus a few
crates under MPL-2.0, used unmodified. Each package ships its own licence text;
`cargo metadata` lists the crates of `Cargo.lock`, and `package-lock.json` gives the
licence of each npm package.
